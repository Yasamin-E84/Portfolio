About Me

Who I Am

I'm Yasamin Soraghi, a 19-year-old developer passionate about creating beautiful, functional web experiences. My journey in web development started with frontend technologies, and I've been continuously expanding my skills into new areas ever since.

My Journey

I began with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, focusing on building clean, responsive interfaces. As I progressed, I realized the importance of understanding the full stack, which led me to explore backend development with Python and PHP. I'm also passionate about WordPress development and motion graphics, using tools like After Effects, Photoshop, and Illustrator to bring designs to life.

My philosophy is to build strong fundamentals before moving to frameworks. That's why I focus on vanilla JavaScript and core web technologies, ensuring I understand how things work under the hood. This foundation makes learning new tools and frameworks much easier and more meaningful.

What I Do

Frontend Development: I create responsive, accessible web interfaces using HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. I focus on semantic markup, modern CSS techniques, and performance optimization.

Backend Learning: I'm actively learning Python and PHP to understand server-side development, APIs, and how the web works behind the scenes.

WordPress: I build custom WordPress themes and plugins, combining my frontend skills with PHP knowledge to create flexible, maintainable WordPress solutions.

Motion Graphics: I create dynamic animations and motion graphics using After Effects, bringing static designs to life with purpose and style.

My Approach

I believe in writing clean, maintainable code that's both functional and beautiful. I prioritize accessibility, performance, and user experience in everything I build. Whether it's a website, a WordPress theme, or a motion graphic, I aim to create work that serves a purpose and makes a positive impact.

I'm always learning and growing, exploring new technologies and techniques. My goal is to become a well-rounded full-stack developer while maintaining my passion for creating beautiful, user-centered experiences.

Let's Connect

I'm always open to discussing projects, collaborations, or just chatting about web development and design. Feel free to reach out through any of my contact channels!
