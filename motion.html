Motion Graphics

I create dynamic, engaging motion graphics using After Effects, Photoshop, and Illustrator. From animated logos to product launches, I combine design principles with animation techniques to bring static designs to life with purpose and style.

My Approach

  • Purpose-Driven Animation: Every motion serves a purpose, enhancing the message rather than distracting from it.
  • After Effects Mastery: Using After Effects for complex animations, transitions, and cinematic effects.
  • Design First: Creating assets in Illustrator and Photoshop before animating, ensuring high-quality visuals.
  • Smooth Transitions: Focusing on easing, timing, and flow to create natural, pleasing animations.
  • Brand Consistency: Ensuring motion graphics align with brand identity and design systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of motion graphics do you create?
I create a variety of motion graphics including animated logos, product launch videos, brand identity animations, social media content, and UI animations. Each project is tailored to the specific needs and brand identity of the client.
Do you work with video editing?
Yes, I use After Effects for both motion graphics and video editing. I can handle projects from concept to final export, including color grading and sound design integration.
Can you create web animations?
While I primarily work in After Effects for video content, I also understand how to translate motion design principles to web animations using CSS and JavaScript, creating cohesive experiences across different media.
